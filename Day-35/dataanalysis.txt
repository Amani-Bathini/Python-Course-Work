## What is Data Analysis?
Data Analysis is the process of **examining, cleaning, organizing, and interpreting data** to find useful information and make better decisions.

## Why do we need Data Analysis?
* To understand data
* To identify patterns and trends
* To find problems
* To make better decisions
* To predict future outcomes
* To take appropriate actions

## Types of Data Analysis
__________________________________
### 1. Descriptive Analysis
Descriptive analysis focuses on understanding and summarizing past and present data.
**Question:** What happened?
**Purpose:** Understand and summarize past data.

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis
Diagnostic analysis goes one step further.
**Question:** Why did it happen?
**Purpose:** Find the reasons or causes behind an event.

### 3. Predictive Analysis
Predictive analysis uses historical data, statistical techniques, and AI/ML models to estimate what is likely to happen in the future.
**Question:** What will happen?
**Purpose:** Predict future outcomes using **historical data, statistics, and AI/ML models**.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis
Prescriptive analysis goes beyond prediction.
It uses data, predictions, rules, optimization, and sometimes AI to recommend what action should be taken.
**Question:** What should we do?
**Purpose:** Suggest the best actions or plans based on data and predictions.
